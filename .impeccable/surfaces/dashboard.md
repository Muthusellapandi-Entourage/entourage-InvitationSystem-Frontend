# Dashboard

Mode: operate

Audience: an Entourage administrator at a desk, signing in to create and review events.
Job: reach a real event record quickly and leave a shell later modules can occupy.
Task: sign in, scan the tally, open or create an event, edit its specification, leave.
Constraints: the brief pins the sidebar shell, the neutral palette, one accent, and both themes. Invitation, badge, guest, and campaign modules stay out.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Events are lines in a typesetter's galley, and an opened event is its job specification. The page refuses a card grid and refuses icon-stat tiles as its structure.

OWN-WORLD: A stepped neutral ramp from warm paper to charcoal, with sheet, rule, and ink kept apart in both themes. One spruce accent is reserved for the primary action, the signed-in focus ring, and the active status stamp. Corners stay at 6px. Overlays are the only shadows. One grotesque carries every label; dates and counts are tabular.

STORY: The administrator signs in, reads the day's tally, and treats the event list as the work. Opening a row shows a ruled specification, not a brochure. They can trust that disabled modules are honestly not built yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 248px sidebar holds the wordmark, two nav groups, and the signed-in person. The content column opens with the greeting, then a three-part tally divided by hairlines, then a full-width recent-events table. The create action lives on Events, aligned to the page title.

FORM: Typesetter's galley with a specification rail, fourth grounded direction. Seed key 9f5ad953. Palette and chrome are translated to the brief's charcoal, paper, and single accent. Exposure-record raise: the neutral ramp is stepped so dark mode is designed, not inverted. Boarding-pass raise: dates, counts, and status stay tabular and hold their place when they change.

FINISH: reviewed, disposition ship for the scored fixes, documented in DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json
